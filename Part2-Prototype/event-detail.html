Highveld Spring Half Marathon

Road 21.1km Centurion, Gauteng Sat, 21 Nov 2026 · 06:30

About this event

The Highveld Spring Half Marathon is a fast, flat 21.1km road race that winds through the leafy suburbs of Centurion. Ideal for runners chasing a personal best, the route is fully marshalled and water points are positioned every 3km.

All finishers receive a commemorative medal, a technical event tee and a timing chip result. A dedicated 10km and a 5km family event run alongside the main half marathon, making it a great morning out for the whole club.

What's included

  • ✓ Chip-timed, official result and finish photo
  • ✓ Finisher medal and technical race tee
  • ✓ Secure bag drop, water points and medical support
